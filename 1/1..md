Algorithm: Posting a New Photo on Instagram





* open insta on ur phone.
* Log in to your Instagram account if you are not.
* Look at the top left of the screen and find the + button.
* Tap on the + button.
* Choose the photo that you want to post .
* Tap Next.
* Write something about the photo in the caption box.
* Check the photo and caption once before posting.
* Tap the Share button.
* photo uploaded.
* When the upload is complete, the photo will appear on your Instagram profile.
* The photo has now been posted successfully.
