 Algorithm: Sending a Message on WhatsApp

1. open your phone.
2. open the WhatsApp app.
3. Find the person and send a message.
4. Tap on that person's name.
5. A chat screen will open.
6. Tap on the message box at the bottom.
7. Type the message you want to send.
8. Check the message once.
9. Tap the send button.
10. The message will be sent .

Step That Can Be Misunderstood

The step "Find the person" is not very clear.

Clearer Version

Instead of writing:

"Find the person."

We can write:

"Tap the search icon at the top of WhatsApp, type the person's name in the search box, and tap their name ."
